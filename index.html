TIFR Centre for Applicable Mathematics · Bengaluru, India

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Centre for Applicable Mathematics, Bengaluru. My research focuses on control theory for partial differential equations, particularly controllability and stabilization of nonlinear, coupled, and fluid-related PDE systems.

My work brings together functional analysis, spectral and moment methods, Carleman estimates, and techniques from geometric control.

Research

Control theory & partial differential equations

My interests lie at the interface of mathematical analysis, dynamics and control of PDEs. I study when and how evolving systems can be driven toward desired states through suitably designed controls.

Null controllabilityStabilizationCarleman estimatesMoment methodsSpectral inequalitiesFluid systemsGeometric controlFourth-order PDEs

Boundary and interior control

Parabolic, dispersive and coupled PDE systems, including Kuramoto–Sivashinsky and Kawahara-type models.

Stabilization of evolution equations

Feedback stabilization, backstepping methods and controlled coupled systems.

Nonlinear and geometric methods

Approximate controllability and finite-dimensional Fourier-mode forcing.

Fluid-related systems

Compressible Navier–Stokes, fluid–structure interaction and related equations.
